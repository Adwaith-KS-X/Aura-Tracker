# Food data

USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy (April 2018) and FNDDS 2021–2023 (October 31, 2024). Retrieved November 6, 2026. https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/

USDA data are CC0/public domain: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/#bkmk-9 . These selected, normalized records retain FDC IDs for source lookup. All values are per 100 g. Rows missing energy or any macro, or outside basic physical bounds, were excluded. Duplicate exact names were removed.

The catalog contains 10,000 foods and ingredients, NOT 10,000 Indian dishes. 135 Indian dish/food entries are prioritised, including 104 original Aura illustrative recipe estimates. These recipes use USDA ingredient values and assumed finished-batch yields; see recipe-provenance.json. They are explicitly labelled estimates, not lab-tested or individually dietitian-reviewed. No third-party Indian recipe prose or restricted recipe datasets are redistributed.

Oil, preparation, water absorption and portion measurement can materially change values. Match raw versus cooked foods carefully. No allergen or dietary-safety claims are inferred. No medical nutrition recommendations are generated.
